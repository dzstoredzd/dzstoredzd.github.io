🔒 Optional Google Drive backup

Keep a safe copy of your shop in your own Google Drive.

A lost or broken phone shouldn't mean a lost business. With one tap you can sign in with Google and back up your data to a private folder in your own Google Drive, then restore it on a new device.

  • Daily automatic backups, kept on your device by default
  • Optional one-tap cloud backup to your Google Drive
  • Restore everything on a new phone or PC in minutes
🛡️ Store Soft uses the limited drive.appdata permission. It can only read and write its own hidden backup folder — it can never see, open or touch any of your other Google Drive files. Backups live in your account; you can delete them and revoke access anytime. See our Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Still have a question? Reach us on WhatsApp or by email — we answer like humans, not tickets.

Do I need the internet to use Store Soft?

No. Selling, stock, debt and reports work without a connection. Internet is only needed for optional services such as Sync, Google Drive backup, or downloading an update.

Is my shop data sent to any server?

The base product keeps business data on your device. If you optionally enable multi-device Sync, the business data needed for synchronization is sent to our secured Supabase backend. Optional Google Drive backups go to your own private Drive app folder.

What does the Google sign-in access?

Only a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, using the limited drive.appdata permission. Store Soft cannot see or open any of your other Drive files, photos or documents. You can revoke access and delete the backups at any time from your Google account.

How much does it cost?

The 7-day trial is free. The base license is 7,000 DA paid once for the main device. Optional Sync costs 3,000 DA per year for each additional device.

Which languages are supported?

Arabic (with full right-to-left layout), French and English. You can switch language in the settings.
