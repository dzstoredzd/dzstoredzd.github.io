Delete your account and data

Last updated: 26 July 2026

This page explains how to delete your Store Soft data and, if you have one, your Store Soft account. There are two separate things you can delete, and you can do either one without the other: your business data (products, sales, customers, debts…) and your account (the email and password you sign in with for multi-device sync).

Most people do not have an account. Store Soft works fully offline with no account and no sign-in. You only have an account if you subscribed to the optional multi-device sync tier and created one with an email and password. If you never did that, nothing of yours is stored on our servers — skip to Deleting the data on your device.

Option 1 — Delete your business data in the app

The app can erase all of your business data by itself. This is the fastest way, and it does not need our help.

  1. Open Store Soft and sign in as the owner.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the danger zone at the bottom of the page.
  4. Choose Clear all business data and confirm.

The app takes a backup snapshot first, then deletes. If your shop uses multi-device sync, the deletion is applied to every device in your store, not just the one you are holding — the other devices empty themselves the next time they sync.

What this removes

  • Products, categories, barcodes and units
  • Stock lots, stock movements and price history
  • Sales and receipts
  • Customers and their credit / debt records (الكريدي)
  • Suppliers and their debt records
  • Cash sessions, cash movements and expenses
  • Repair tickets and their parts

What this keeps

  • Your user accounts inside the app (owner and staff logins)
  • Your app settings (language, shop name, printing…)
  • Your licence key
  • Your Store Soft account, if you have one — use Option 2 for that
If the button refuses to run. On a shop that uses multi-device sync, this action needs to reach our server before it deletes anything, so that the deletion can reach your other devices too. It will stop and change nothing if the device is offline, if your subscription has expired, or if the device has been removed from the store. If you are in any of those situations, use Option 2 below and we will delete everything for you — or simply uninstall the app to remove the copy on your device.

Option 2 — Delete your whole account

To delete your Store Soft account and everything stored on our servers, send us a request. We do this by email because we need to confirm the request really comes from the account owner.

Email younes.mimene@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”, and include:

  • The email address of the account — please send the request from that same address so we can verify it is you
  • Your store code (the 6-character code from Settings → Sync), if you still have access to it — this helps us find your store faster

You can also reach us on WhatsApp, but we will still confirm the deletion by email to the account address.

How long it takes. We action deletion requests within 30 days, and normally much sooner. We will email you to confirm once it is done. You do not need to keep the app installed while you wait.

What gets deleted, and what we keep

Deleted when you delete your account

  • Your sign-in credentials (email address and password)
  • Your store record and its subscription status
  • The list of devices enrolled in your store
  • Every business record synced to our servers — products, sales, customers, suppliers, debts, cash and stock history

What we keep, and why

  • Anonymous usage and crash statistics. The app sends anonymous counts (installs, opens) and stripped crash reports that identify a device only by a one-way hashed value. These carry no name, email or business data, and there is no way to trace them back to you or to reverse the hash — so they cannot be found and removed once your account is gone. They stay in our aggregate statistics.
  • Payment and invoicing records. If you paid for a sync subscription, we may keep the record of that payment where accounting or tax law requires it. It is kept for that purpose only.
Deleting your account does not erase the app on your phone or PC. Store Soft is offline-first, so your shop's data also lives on your own device. After the account is deleted, sync simply stops. To remove the local copy, follow the step below.

Deleting the data on your device

Your business data is stored on your own device and never leaves it unless you turn on Drive backup or sync. To remove that local copy, do either of these:

  • Use Settings → danger zone → Clear all business data in the app (see Option 1), or
  • Uninstall Store Soft. On Android, uninstalling the app deletes its local database with it. On Windows, uninstall the app and delete its data folder.

Deleting your Google Drive backups

If you turned on the optional Google Drive backup, your backup files are in your own Google Drive, in a hidden app folder — we have no access to them and cannot delete them for you. You can remove them from inside the app (Settings → Backup), or turn off the backup and disconnect Store Soft from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
